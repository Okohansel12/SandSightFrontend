    What this model can and can't do

    • Best on wells with an established, continuous sand-reading history — not usable for new or unmonitored wells.
    • Accuracy improves with more production history; predictions below 6 months of input rely partly on field-median imputation.
    • Trained and validated on 8 OML 30 fields, 1966–2011, with no bottomhole pressure data (98%+ missing across all fields).
    • Well-level petrophysical and seismic data was tested and did not meaningfully improve accuracy — coverage is limited to ~6% of records.
    This is a screening and prioritization aid, not a substitute for monitoring. It is weakest exactly where the stakes are highest — severe sanding events — so results should inform engineering judgment, not replace it.
